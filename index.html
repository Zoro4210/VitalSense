The Problem

Prevention today is
reactive, not predictive.

Pressure ulcers (bedsores) are one of healthcare's most common, costly, and preventable complications yet prevention still relies on manual repositioning schedules and caregiver vigilance.

  • Nurses and family caregivers can't monitor posture and pressure 24/7
  • By the time redness is visible, tissue injury has often already begun
  • Each hospital-acquired injury adds $11,000-$22,000 in treatment cost
  • Small hospitals and home caregivers have the least support and the highest risk
12.8% of hospitalized adults 26.6% in ICUs globally $27B+ annual U.S. burden $1.3B market in 2026
How It Works

Two Modules. One System.

Sensing happens on the body and the bed. All AI inference happens locally, on-device no cloud round-trip, no latency, no privacy compromise.

System Brain

Module 1 Smart Belt

Worn at the waist. Continuously tracks patient posture and acts as the system's brain running all Edge AI inference locally with no cloud dependency.

  • Silicon Labs EFR32xG26 with built-in IMU & on-chip AI accelerator
  • Tracks posture: left / supine / right in real time
  • Communicates with the Bedsheet over BLE
Pressure Sensing
Risk Score LOW Safe

Module 2 Smart Bedsheet

Four detachable, repositionable sensing plates that measure pressure distribution across the bed. Stays idle until the Belt requests a reading maximizing battery life.

  • Force Sensitive Resistors (FSR) built around an ESP32
  • Measures pressure, weight concentration & movement
  • Wake-on-demand power model for efficiency

The Signal Flow From Posture to Risk Score

1
Belt monitors posture Continuously via the onboard IMU left, supine, or right
2
Posture unchanged? Belt requests pressure data from the Bedsheet over BLE
3
Bedsheet wakes up Reads all four FSR plates and sends data back to Belt
4
EFR32 fuses all data Position + time + pressure on-device AI inference
5
Risk score output
Low Medium High
6
Score is pushed To the mobile app or hospital dashboard in real time
Key Capabilities

Engineered for Real Care

On-Device Edge AI

All risk inference runs locally on the EFR32xG26's AI accelerator. No internet needed, no cloud costs, no latency data never leaves the device.

Continuous Posture Tracking

The Smart Belt's IMU continuously detects whether the patient is on their left side, supine, or right triggering pressure checks only when needed.

4-Zone Pressure Mapping

Four repositionable FSR plates map pressure distribution, weight concentration, and micro-movement across the bed surface with granular precision.

Real-Time Risk Alerts

Low / Medium / High risk scores are pushed instantly to the mobile app or hospital dashboard flagging high-risk patients before damage begins.

Home Care Ready

Designed for family caregivers managing a bedridden patient at home. Simple installation, affordable price point, and a mobile-first experience.

Hospital Dashboard

Centralized multi-bed monitoring for small hospitals. High-risk patients are automatically surfaced no expensive proprietary infrastructure required.
